# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS, no build step. Chosen so each design-skill trial in this repo differs only by the skill, not the tooling.

## Users

Primary: people commissioning hand-made İznik tiles: homeowners, café/hotel owners and architects planning a wall, kitchen, fountain or panel. They are deciding whether this atelier is worth the trip to choose patterns and colours in person.

## Product Purpose

Kobalt Çini is a (fictional, demo) çini atelier in İznik that makes quartz-body tiles and plates by hand, to order, and runs Saturday workshops. Success for the site: the visitor comes to the atelier to see the work and choose in person.

## Positioning

Everything is drawn and painted by hand in İznik, in the traditional order (body, tahrir outline, colours, glaze, kiln), and made to order rather than sold off a shelf.

## Operating Context

Orders start with a visit: seeing real samples, colours under daylight, and agreeing size and pattern. Production takes about three weeks. Workshops are Saturdays, 10.00–16.00, six people.

## Evidence on Hand

No photographs, testimonials, press or customer list exist. Do not invent them; illustrative artwork must be authored and read as illustration.

## Product Principles

1. Get the visitor to the atelier: address, hours and how to get there are never more than one step away.
2. Show the making, not adjectives about it.
3. Speak plainly in Turkish, like the person behind the counter.
